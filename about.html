Our story

Leveling the cybersecurity playing field

In a digital world where threats evolve faster than most businesses can react, Zeogital was born with a singular mission: to level the cybersecurity playing field. We saw startups and SMEs being left behind — vulnerable, under-protected, and overwhelmed by jargon-heavy solutions built for giants.

We deliver enterprise-grade, layered security that's tailored, scalable, and human-friendly. Our approach blends strategic consulting with cutting-edge technology, giving growing businesses the confidence to innovate without fear. Whether you're launching your first app or scaling across markets, Zeogital is your silent guardian, your digital shield, your partner in resilience.

Vision

The most trusted partner for SMEs worldwide

To be the most trusted cybersecurity partner for SMEs worldwide, delivering enterprise-grade, layered cybersecurity that empowers businesses to operate confidently in a connected world — with innovation, integrity, and unmatched reliability.

Mission

Enterprise sophistication, SME accessibility

To provide small and medium-sized enterprises with advanced, multi-layered cybersecurity solutions that rival the sophistication of large corporations — integrating cutting-edge technology, proactive threat intelligence, and expert consulting to protect web applications, data, and digital assets against evolving threats.

Meet the founder

Rev. Kennedy Uwadia Nosazeogie

Founder & Proprietor, Zeogital Consulting

Rev. Nosazeogie brings a rare combination of deep financial expertise, economic insight, and cybersecurity strategy, with a career spanning finance, academia, and technology. A graduate of Economics and Statistics from the University of Benin, he held senior roles in treasury management, business consulting, and university lecturing before pivoting into cybersecurity, honing his craft under the mentorship of Mr. Nelson Ojovbo at Hagital Consulting Ltd.

With over two decades of multidisciplinary experience in business start-ups, reengineering, risk management, and strategic planning, he understands the operational and financial pressures SMEs face from the inside. That business foundation is fortified by hands-on expertise across SIEM and cloud security, digital forensics, and penetration testing — allowing him to go beyond fixing technical vulnerabilities to delivering holistic solutions that protect a business's core assets and safeguard its dreams.
